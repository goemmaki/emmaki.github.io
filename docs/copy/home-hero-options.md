## Ground rules from the session

- Voice for the above-the-fold pair: Laura Belgray. Conversational, specific, one beat of wit, then a plain statement of the job.
- Two tests (CXL): would anyone click on the headline plus a button alone? Would you say these exact words to a friend?
- Don't sound like a commodity; if a competitor could use the line, it fails.
- One robot joke at most. AI and automation are real services, not a gag.
- No "twenty years" lines (owner's call, 2026-11-03).
