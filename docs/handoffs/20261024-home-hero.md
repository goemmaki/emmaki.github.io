# Session handoff: home hero and site shell

## Owner goals and decisions

Goal: a Swiss-style, poster-like home hero for the emmaki Fiverr portfolio site, built to the site's design system (`docs/design-system/`, especially `DESIGN.md` and `DESIGN-HANDOFF.md`). Reference: the owner's "EYES WILL / FIRST GO HERE. / AND THEN HERE. | small text" poster, which is three tiers on one left axis.

Approved on 2026-10-24 (variant "F" in the lab):

- A **rectangle** whose height is set by a rotated "Hello!" (reads bottom to top), running from the cap height of "ANTI-SLOP" to the baseline of "HELP YOU?".
- Tier 1, "ANTI-SLOP / OPS HERE.", is Geist 600 capitals, -0.03em tracking, filling the text column exactly.
- Tier 2, "HOW CAN I / HELP YOU?", is two lines. **OPS axis** (chosen 2026-10-25): its H stems sit on the ink of the A's foot and its widest line ends on the ink of the S of OPS, both measured from glyph ink.
- The H1, "Easily startled copywriter by day, *vituperative* Luddite fighting Skynet's patterns by night." (2026-10-25), sits beside the question and starts under the H of HERE, so the gap matches the headline's word space. It is justified ("a bento box, but with words"; a short last line is fine), fitted to the question's cap-to-baseline height at the best-fit size, sentence case, Geist 500, white. Below 16px it drops under the rectangle.
- No "I'm an" before the H1: the owner tried it and felt it weakens the copy. It answers "who are you" like an introduction, while the bare line reads as a character description with an old-school superhero cadence.
- Rejected on 2026-10-25: boxes or animation round the question (not Swiss typography; nothing moves while the look is being settled), line-by-line scaled H1 lines, the swapped layout, and the H axis (question sized to end one gap before HERE). The full test log is in Studio `docs/research/hero-type-fitting-20261025.md`.
- No rule between the question and the H1 (the owner disliked it). No yellow full stop (also disliked).
- Monochrome headline: "ANTI-SLOP" is in the headline colour, not yellow. The wordmark is white in dark mode.
- Sparkles on "vituperative" are brand yellow in dark mode and amber-orange (`#E07B00`, `#E86A00`, ≥3:1 on white) in light mode. Never brown, never green ("slop colour").
- The header hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up or keyboard focus, and is translucent with blur. The owner asked whether this fits the design; the agreed answer is yes, provided it stays restrained (70% canvas, light blur, no bottom rule: the owner had the faint scrolled rule removed on 2026-10-25).
- The menu has no numbers.
- The shell steps from 1200px to 1400px at 1600px and up. On big screens the hero centres vertically and is capped by viewport height. Empty space is fine; the owner plans a side-scroll or kinetic-type section straight after the hero.
- Copy is the owner's. Don't change or add words.

- H1 fitting: `src/app/hero-lab/rect-fit.tsx`. It sizes the question (`--r`) to the S of OPS and the gap to the H of HERE from canvas glyph ink, sets the question's left margin to the A's foot, binary-searches the largest H1 size that fits the question's cap-to-baseline height, then searches up to 15% smaller for the size whose justified lines stretch least (best fit; leading capped at 1.5), and stretches line-height so the last baselines meet. Below 16px it sets `data-stacked`. Measured on 2026-10-25: the inks meet within 1px at 1440, 1024 and 768.
- Sizing maths: the "Temporary hero at /hero-lab" block in `src/app/globals.css`. The headline size S solves `(W - gap - cap*rowGap/kH) / (kW + cap*(cap + lead + qBlock*r)/kH)` with measured constants (cap 0.72; "ANTI-SLOP" 5.032em; "Hello!" ink 2.205em; "HELP YOU?" 5.108em). The frame's height cap uses rectangle height ≈ 0.383 × width + 36px.
