## Owner goals and decisions

Goal: a Swiss International Style, poster-like home hero for the emmaki Fiverr portfolio site, built to `docs/design-system/` (`DESIGN.md`, `DESIGN-HANDOFF.md`), followed by a full-viewport section that scrolls sideways.

Hero, approved (lab variant "F · OPS axis", 2026-10-25):

- Typographic only. **Every word sits inside one invisible rectangle**: the rotated "Hello!" (reads bottom to top) sets the height, from the cap height of "ANTI-SLOP" to the baseline of "HELP YOU?"; "ANTI-SLOP" sets the width. No boxes, rules, fills or animation.
- Tier 1, "ANTI-SLOP / OPS HERE.", Geist 600 capitals, -0.03em, fills the column. Monochrome (no yellow, no yellow full stop).
- Tier 2, "HOW CAN I / HELP YOU?": its H stems sit on the ink of the A's foot and its widest line ends on the ink of the S of OPS.
- The H1, "Easily startled copywriter by day, *vituperative* Luddite fighting Skynet's patterns by night.", starts under the H of HERE, is justified ("a bento box, but with words"; a short last line is fine), and is fitted to tier 2's cap-to-baseline height. "vituperative" is Source Serif light italic with sparkles: brand yellow in dark mode, amber-orange (`#E07B00`, `#E86A00`) in light mode; never brown or green.
- No "I'm an" before the H1: the owner feels it weakens the copy (an introduction, where the bare line is a character description with a superhero cadence).
- The hero runs under the translucent header and stops `--peek` short of the fold so the next section peeks in. The owner chose peeking over a scroll cue.
- Header: hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up or focus, translucent with blur, **no bottom rule** (removed 2026-10-25). Menu has no numbers. The shell is now 1440px with a fluid gutter (`b1969429`; see `docs/design-system/blog-post.md`).
- Copy is the owner's. Don't change or add words; use obvious placeholders.

Nib section, **not approved; the owner said to leave it alone for now** (2026-10-25):

- Idea, from the owner's Pinterest reference (`/mnt/eliteg6_docswap/accountant/Screenshot 2026-10-24 122750.png`, ink narrowing down into a fountain-pen nib): the hero's canvas carries on as flat, angular ink that narrows into a nib whose point lands on the next section's headline. That section is a full viewport that scrolls sideways, perhaps with cards (undecided).
- The owner disliked the current build: the nib is "weird shaped" and on the left. Agreed diagnosis: it should be **centred** like the reference, the ink should narrow **evenly from both sides**, the nib needs real shoulders (flare, then taper to the point) instead of a square block and a spike, and the shape shouldn't warp with width (the clip-path mixes % points with a fixed neck).
- Colours are open. Claude's recommendation: inverse paper (ink stays the hero canvas colour; white paper in dark mode, black in light) and yellow only as a small accent such as the breather hole, because a full #FCC300 field glares against black, `DESIGN.md` allows solid yellow only as a rare splash, and a white nib on yellow is unreadable in light mode. The owner hasn't picked.
- Later (owner's plan, "tomorrow"): slight parallax, blurred splotches or scattered letters while scrolling, and possibly vertical scroll driving the sideways strip.
